01 / GAME ATLAS

Same internet.
Different environments.

Games can help people create, connect and learn. Their design also changes the risks players encounter. Compare the tools with the limits, not just the game title.

Showing all four game evaluations.

R

01 / User-generated social games

Roblox

Parent-managed content and chatAge-based settings and reportingSpending controls

STRENGTH

Linked parental controls let families manage content maturity, communication and spending. Age-based account settings and reporting tools help reduce unwanted contact.

LIMITATION

User-created experiences vary enormously, and controls cannot catch every unsafe interaction. Review account settings and the specific experiences being played.

OUR EVALUATION

Flexible controls are valuable, but moderation of the huge catalogue of experiences is just as important.

Research evidence & sources

In October 2026, eSafety reported that Roblox used tools to detect harmful user-created experiences after publication. This illustrates why detection and response speed matter.

F

02 / Competitive multiplayer

Fortnite

Chat and friend permissionsRestricted account featuresSpending and reporting tools

STRENGTH

Epic offers friend, voice and text-chat permissions, spending settings and reporting. Cabined Accounts restrict certain features until parental consent.

LIMITATION

Competitive play can still involve hostile communication. Account permissions and in-game reporting settings determine which protections are available.

OUR EVALUATION

Strong permission tools and creator-content review help; accessible reporting and effective moderation remain essential.

Research evidence & sources

eSafety's October 2026 report describes Epic's review of Fortnite Islands before publication, while also noting that detection and response practices across services varied.

Epic parental controls ↗ · Cabined Accounts ↗
M

03 / Creative sandbox / multiplayer servers

Minecraft

Multiplayer / Realms permissionsJava player reportingChat filtering in supported contexts

STRENGTH

Family settings can manage multiplayer and Realms access. Minecraft: Java Edition has player reporting, and some multiplayer contexts use chat filtering.

LIMITATION

Safety differs between private worlds, public servers, game editions and modifications. Some third-party servers are not proactively moderated by Mojang.

OUR EVALUATION

Playing in a trusted private world can reduce exposure to unknown people, but server governance and edition differences matter.

Research evidence & sources

eSafety's October 2026 report highlights that third-party Minecraft worlds were not all proactively moderated by Mojang. This makes server choice a meaningful safety factor.

Minecraft parental controls ↗ · Minecraft player safety ↗
A

04 / Social deduction / lobby-based play

Among Us

Quick Chat / permitted Free ChatLanguage filtersIn-game reporting

STRENGTH

Quick Chat can limit typed communication to preset phrases. Players can use chat options, language filters and in-game reporting.

LIMITATION

Preset chat does not prevent every form of unkind behaviour. Open lobbies and permitted free-text chat can still expose players to unwanted interactions.

OUR EVALUATION

Communication restrictions are useful, particularly in a chat-driven game, but reporting, enforcement and trusted play groups remain important.

Research evidence & sources

Among Us was not one of the four services in eSafety's October 2026 transparency report. Its evaluation here is based on Innersloth's documented tools and their practical limitations.

How developers can go further

Test controls with children and families, support accessible reporting and different languages, measure response times, audit moderation quality, make purchases transparent, and give people meaningful control over personal data. Safety should be part of development and testing, not an afterthought.

05 / TAKEAWAY

Protect the fun, too.

01 / PLAYER

Make smart choices.

Keep personal details private. Use privacy settings, be respectful, take breaks and ask a trusted adult for help.

02 / DEVELOPER

Design for safety.

Prevent harmful contact, offer accessible tools, moderate effectively and learn from reports.

03 / COMMUNITY

Support each other.

Stop unwanted contact. Save relevant evidence when safe, report appropriately and seek support. For immediate danger, get urgent help.
